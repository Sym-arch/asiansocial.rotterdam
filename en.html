    01 — Concept

    You don't come to study Japanese.
    You come because it looks fun.

    En. is a place to enjoy Japanese food, sport and culture together — and to let nationality and language quietly stop mattering while you do it.

    日本の文化・食・スポーツを一緒に楽しみながら、国籍や言語を越えて 人が自然につながっていく場所です。

    1. You join because it looks interesting.面白そうだから参加する。
    2. You come back because it was fun.楽しいからまた来る。
    3. And somewhere along the way, you made friends.気づいたら友達ができている。

    Vision

    Different people,
    one table.

    国籍や言葉を越えて、人と人がつながる街をつくる。

    Not “because you are a foreigner” or “because you are Japanese” — a community where you simply meet as one person.

    Mission

    Create reasons to connect.

    一緒に楽しむ「きっかけ」をつくる。

    Sports

    Move together

    一緒に動く。

    Playing beats talking. Nobody is a stranger after a game.

    Meat

    Eat together

    一緒に食べる。

    Yakiniku, izakaya, a grill in someone's garden. A table does the work.

    Culture

    Experience together

    一緒に体験する。

    Festivals, films, crafts, seasons — things you try rather than watch.

    Language is not the purpose. Connection is. 「言語交換」をするのではなく、人と人が出会う理由をつくる。

    Values

    Five things we hold to

    1. 01

      Be curious

      違いを楽しむ。

      New people, new cultures, new opinions. Different is what makes it interesting.

    2. 02

      Do together

      一緒にやる。

      We don't start with conversation. We start with doing something. Experience first. Connection follows.

    3. 03

      Come as you are

      ありのままで参加する。

      Come alone. Come with shaky English. Come for the first time. All fine.

    4. 04

      People first

      イベントより、人を大切にする。

      Running events is not the goal. The people you met that night are. The people are the point.

    5. 05

      Make it fun

      まず、自分たちが楽しむ。

      Better a rough idea that sounds fun than a perfect event nobody enjoys.
