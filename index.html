    Membership

    Members get in earlier, and cheaper

    There is no fee and never will be. Booking any event already makes you one — this is just what it gets you.

    • Member price on tickets

      Paid events carry a member price. It is shown on the event page the moment you are signed in.

    • First to know

      New events reach members before they go anywhere else — Meetup and Instagram come later.

    • Buy before it sells out

      Booking opens to members first, so the small evenings do not disappear before you have seen them.

    • Coupons from 2027

      Member coupons at partner restaurants and venues in Rotterdam. Not running yet — we are building it.
