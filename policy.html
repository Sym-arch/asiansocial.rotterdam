    Policy

    Event policy and privacy

    Last updated: November 14, 2026

    Who we are

    Asian Socials is run by Asian Socials, a community organisation. Representative: Taiyo Iino. Contact: info@asiansocials.nl

    Tickets & entry

    • We check tickets at the entrance. Each ticket can be used once — if the same ticket is shown twice, only the first person gets in.
    • Every event has a capacity. If it's full, people with a ticket are let in first — if you don't have one, you may not be able to join. Please book ahead to be sure of your spot.
    • You must be 18 or older. We may ask for ID.

    At the event

    • Everyone is welcome, whatever their nationality, background, gender, sexuality, religion, age or language level.
    • Harassment, discrimination, unwanted advances or aggressive behaviour are not tolerated. We'll ask you to leave, with no refund, and you won't be able to join future events.
    • No selling, promoting or recruiting — including business pitches, network marketing, and religious or political recruitment — without our permission.
    • Please follow the venue's rules and our staff's directions. We may refuse entry or ask anyone to leave who doesn't.
    • Drink responsibly.
    • Look after your belongings. We're not responsible for anything lost, stolen or damaged.
    • If you have food allergies, tell us before the event. We can't guarantee food is free of allergens.

    Cancellations & refunds

    • Tickets are non-refundable. This includes not being able to come, changing your mind, arriving late or not showing up.
    • Transfer: Can't make it? Send your ticket screenshot to a friend — then your friend can come to the event in your place.
    • Move to another event: Email info@asiansocials.nl at least 48 hours before the event starts. We'll keep your booking as credit for 2 months — tell us which event you'd like when one opens. Credit can't be exchanged for cash.

    If we cancel or change an event

    • Some events need a minimum number of participants. If an event doesn't reach it, we'll cancel it and let you know by email — usually 5 days before, but sometimes closer to the date.
    • We may also cancel or change an event because of weather, venue problems, safety or other circumstances beyond our control.
    • If we cancel, or change the date, time or venue and you can't make it, you can choose a full refund to the card you paid with, or credit for another event (valid for 2 months). Email info@asiansocials.nl to tell us which you'd prefer.

    Privacy

    • Asian Socials is responsible for your data. We keep your name, email address, member number and bookings, to run bookings, send tickets and tell you about changes to events.
    • Your profile also has optional fields (nationality, city, date of birth) to help us plan where and what kind of events to host. They're entirely optional, and you can change or delete them at any time.
    • We use Supabase (database), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Vercel (hosting) and Google Analytics (visitor statistics) to run this service.
    • Google Analytics tells us how many people visit and which pages they read. We only use analytics cookies if you accept them, and we never use it for advertising. You can change your choice at any time with the cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.
    • We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
    • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time: info@asiansocials.nl
